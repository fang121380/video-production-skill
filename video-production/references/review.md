# Review guidance

Review the exported artifact against the user brief, not only the project timeline. Record what was inspected, how, and what remains unverified.

- Technical: container and codecs, dimensions, frame rate, duration, audio presence, successful decode and nonempty output.
- Story and picture: visual continuity, shot diversity and purpose, framing, transitions, character identity and emotional arc.
- Motion: observe continuous playback where available; inspect consecutive frames around key actions and transitions. Check flicker, timing, limb attachment, foot contact, prop contact and expression changes.
- Sound and captions: check audio against source and verify words and timing by listening when that capability exists. Otherwise label the timing estimated or not audibly verified. Signal processing and transcription are evidence, not proof of listening.
- Delivery: state deviations and quality limits. A successful export, a lint pass, and snapshots alone do not establish smooth motion or satisfactory artistic quality.
